My grooming philosophy is rooted in animal welfare and a pet-first approach. Every session prioritises health, comfort, and safety — not just aesthetics. My goal is for every pet to have a calm, stress-free, and enjoyable grooming experience.
🪢 毛結與解毛政策 Matting & De-matting Policy

嚴重打結的寵物需要額外時間及精力處理，因此將收取額外費用。

風險告知：在解毛過程中，特別是毛結靠近皮膚的情況下，有可能發生意外割傷。本人雖受過專業訓練並盡力預防，但解毛工作本身需使用利器，存在一定風險。

長期積累的毛結會越來越緊，可能勒住皮膚甚至撕裂皮膚，同時藏污納垢，滋生黴菌、細菌，引起皮膚問題。嚴重情況下，可能需要全部剃除。

解毛後，寵物可能出現皮膚痕癢、泛紅、自行抓刮，甚至部分毛髮無法正常再生，或短暫出現行為改變。

定期美容是預防毛結最有效的方法。若您的寵物毛髮情況欠佳，請理解這並非美容師的責任。

Pets with severe matting require additional time and effort, so an extra fee will apply.

Risk Acknowledgment: During dematting, there is a risk of unintentional nicks or cuts, especially where mats are close to the skin. I am highly trained and take every precaution, but dematting involves sharp tools and carries inherent risks.

Mats left untreated grow tighter over time, cutting off circulation and potentially tearing the skin. They can trap moisture and bacteria, causing fungal or skin conditions. In severe cases, the coat may need to be fully shaved down.

After dematting, pets may show itchiness, skin redness, self-inflicted irritation, slower coat regrowth, or brief behavioural changes.

Regular grooming is the best prevention. Please understand that a pet's poor coat condition is not the groomer's responsibility.

本人非常樂意為各年齡層的寵物提供美容服務，但須特別指出：為10歲或以上的年長寵物進行美容，本身存在一定風險。若年長寵物在美容過程中出現突發狀況，本人概不負責。

建議您在預約前，先與您的獸醫商討老齡寵物的健康狀況是否適合接受美容服務。

I love caring for pets of all ages. However, grooming senior pets (10 years and above) carries inherent health risks. I cannot be held responsible if a senior pet passes away or experiences a health event during a grooming session.

I recommend consulting your veterinarian about your senior pet's suitability for grooming before booking.

🤔 Q1 狗狗沖涼後肚痾？你有沒有懷疑過原因？ Q1 Has your dog ever come home from the groomer with an upset stomach?

有可能係狗狗對陌生環境感到緊張，引起腸胃不適；但亦有可能係……寵物店的衛生情況並不如外表般理想。

我曾在寵物店工作，見過唔少令人擔心的內部情況：

・美容工具未經消毒就直接用於下一隻狗

・狗狗便溺後清潔消毒不足，或使用刺激性過強的消毒劑

如果其中一隻狗帶有病毒，其他健康或免疫力較低的狗狗就很容易受到感染。

你的毛孩，值得更安心的環境。

It could be stress from an unfamiliar environment — but it could also be something less visible: the hygiene standards inside some pet salons aren't always what they appear from the outside.

Having worked in pet salons myself, I've seen things that would concern most pet owners:

・Grooming tools used on one dog without proper sanitising before the next

・Inadequate cleaning after toileting accidents, or overly harsh disinfectants used in the space

If one dog carries a virus, healthy dogs — or those with weaker immune systems — can easily be affected.

Your pet deserves a cleaner, calmer, safer experience.

✂️ Q3 同去寵物店有什麼不同？ Q3 What's the difference from a regular salon?

一般澳洲寵物美容店只提供「快靚正」的工廠式服務——而且以下項目通常另外收費：磨甲、擠肛門腺、拔耳毛。

我考取的是日本寵物美容師證照，講求細心、耐心與標準。我可以降低收費跟從澳洲的「基本標準」，但我選擇唔咁做——因為我有自己的原則。

價錢反映質素。我的標準，就係對你毛孩的尊重。

Most Australian grooming salons offer a fast, factory-style service — and many of the following are usually charged as extras: nail filing, anal gland expression, ear hair removal.

I hold a qualified Japanese pet grooming certificate, which emphasises meticulousness, patience, and standards. I could lower my fees to meet Australia's "basic standards," but I chose not to — because I have my own principles.

Price reflects quality. My standard is my respect for your pet.
